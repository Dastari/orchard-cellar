# Orchard & Cellar — 21 September 2026

Release scope: Orchard & Cellar 0.18.0 / Studio 0.9.2. Publication status and live
verification are recorded in the [release record](https://github.com/Dastari/orchard-cellar/pull/44).

- **Explore the refreshed Willowharbour.** Follow the river from an upper lake and animated waterfall to the sea, cross four bridges with stone arches, and explore new gardens, a cobbled square and ten furnished interiors. Fences, hedges and planting now leave clearer paths and gateways.
- **Control the streetlights.** Interact with a lamp to cycle Auto → On → Off. Auto follows sunset and sunrise, and your choice is saved. Lamps now shine brighter.
- **Put pomace back into the garden.** Craft four Pomace and one Fiber into Compost, then apply it once per planting to advance a growing crop by 25% of its required growth.
- **Take preserves on your travels.** All 22 preserved crops now restore Hunger. Eating at full Hunger keeps the portion in your inventory.
- **Earn permanent recipes through village deliveries.** Varied crop, preserve and bottle orders unlock Pantry Lunch and Cellar Supper. New qualifying deliveries count toward these milestones.
- **Harvest fruit without felling the tree.** Mature fruit trees provide fruit and seeds, then ripen again for another harvest.
- **Bring home a Delve keepsake.** Your first victory unlocks the Delver Memorial Planter recipe for your residence.
- **Progression updates.** Fishing now awards Farming XP. East and South house expansions cost 60,000 and 180,000 bronze respectively.
- **Fixes and clearer artwork.** Every hoe tier works with F for tilling, uprooting and restoring soil. Fish swim in active fishing pools again. Crafted fences keep their connected shapes. Hammer, shovel, selected skill and fruit-seed icons are clearer, and Build/Crafting buttons share matching frames.

For Studio creators: larger searchable palettes, clearer pixel tools, right-drag panning, palette drag-and-drop and immediate object previews. Small terrain edits no longer blank the map. Tree artwork, selection panels and shared terrain/fence rendering are improved. Ordinary map edits stay in your local draft until you choose Publish.
