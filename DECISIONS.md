# Decisions have moved to the wiki

The decision register is on the wiki: **https://wiki.orchard.dastari.net/Decisions**.

- Every row of the former `DECISIONS.md` deviation log (214 entries, 2026-08-24 to
  2026-09-22) is on [Decisions/Decision Log](https://wiki.orchard.dastari.net/Decisions/Decision%20Log),
  with its current status (accepted, amended, superseded, resolved, historical).
- Owner decisions and ADRs have one page each under `Decisions/`, listed in the register.
- Questions waiting for the owner are on [Roadmap/Owner Questions](https://wiki.orchard.dastari.net/Roadmap/Owner%20Questions).

To record a new decision or a deviation from a design page, add it to the wiki: a row
in the Decision Log (date · area · decision · reason · supersedes), plus a decision page
for anything an owner decided. Link it from your PR. Do not add entries to this file;
the full former log is in Git history.
